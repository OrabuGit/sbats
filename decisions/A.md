| 2026-11-14 | No launch cost is shown. | DESIGN.md: only with a cited cost per kg, and the data has none. |

| 2026-11-15 | The Earth globe draws NASA GIBS Blue Marble map tiles (Web Mercator, levels 0 to 8) over the bundled Blue Marble texture, using the globe library's own tile engine as a separate layer. | 2026-11-15 | Tiles get the same treatment as the bundled texture (60% less saturation, darker) in the shader. | So the map does not change colour as tiles arrive, and amber stays the brightest thing on it. |
